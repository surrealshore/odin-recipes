Aam Panna

Description

Aam panna is a traditional Indian summer drink made with unripe green mangoes, sugar, salt and spices. It has a balance of sweet, tangy and lightly spiced flavors, and is usually served chilled.

Ingredients

  • Raw Mangoes
  • Sugar or Jaggery
  • Cardamom Powder
  • Roasted cumin powder
  • Black salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Rinse raw, unripe mangoes in water. Place the mangoes in a 2-litre pressure cooker. Add 2 cups of water.
  2. Pressure cook for 2 whistles on medium flame, or for about 8 to 10 minutes.
  3. Gently pour the contents of the cooker into a fine mesh strainer. Place a bowl underneath.
  4. Let the mangoes cool to room temperature. After cooking, the peels should come off easily.
  5. With a sharp-edged spoon or a knife scrape and remove the cooked mango pulp directly in the bowl. Scrape from the peels also.
  6. Collect the cooked mango pulp in a bowl. Discard the peels.
  7. Now add the required amount of sugar or jaggery.
  8. Add 1 teaspoon cardamom powder, 1 teaspoon roasted cumin powder, 2 teaspoons black salt, and 1/4th teaspoon crushed black pepper.
  9. For making aam panna drink, add 2 to 3 tablespoons of aam panna syrup in a glass.
  10. Add chilled water and serve!
